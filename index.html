A Human Story. A Global Mission.

My Story

I am an ordinary person who worked hard to build a livelihood. Together with a business partner, I helped build a project that represented years of effort.

After a painful business loss, I lost an important source of income. Financial pressure affected my family and personal life, including the end of a 22-year marriage.

I share my story with dignity and hope. I want to rebuild stability and turn a difficult experience into an opportunity to help others.

Our Mission

JUST ONE CENT is built on a simple belief: small acts of kindness can make a meaningful difference. Our long-term hope is to support people and families facing hardship.
